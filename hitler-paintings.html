Hitler's Paintings

Hitler mostly painted watercolours of buildings, streets and castles. Critics say they are neat but stiff, like architect's sketches, and he was bad at painting people. The Vienna Academy even said he had more talent for architecture than for painting.

The Courtyard of the Old Residency in Munich, 1914, watercolour by Hitler

Other known paintings: Neuschwanstein Castle, Karlskirche in winter (1912), Ruprechtskirche (1912), the Munich Hofbrauhaus and the Munich town hall. Many 'Hitler paintings' sold today are forgeries.

Some people say history might have changed if the Academy accepted him, but that is just a guess.
