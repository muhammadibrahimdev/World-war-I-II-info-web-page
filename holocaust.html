The Holocaust

The Nazi regime murdered about six million Jews and millions of others, including Roma, disabled people, political prisoners and prisoners of war. It used ghettos, camps like Auschwitz-Birkenau, and mass shootings.

Hungarian Jews arriving at Auschwitz, 1944

We study this so that hatred and racism never lead to something like it again.
