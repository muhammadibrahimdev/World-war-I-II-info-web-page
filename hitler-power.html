Rise to Power

The Great Depression left millions unemployed and the Nazis used propaganda and anger to win votes. On 30 January 1933 Hitler became Chancellor, and soon he was a dictator.

Adolf Hitler, 1930s

He broke the Versailles rules, rebuilt the army, took Austria (1938) and then Czechoslovakia. Britain and France tried 'appeasement' at Munich in 1938 but it failed.
