Life in the Trenches

On the Western Front both sides dug trenches and the line barely moved for years. Soldiers dealt with mud, rats, lice, cold and constant shelling. Many got 'trench foot' from standing in wet boots.

British soldiers on the Somme, July 1916

New weapons

  • Machine guns
  • Poison gas
  • Tanks (from 1916)
  • Aeroplanes and submarines

Battles like Verdun and the Somme in 1916 killed hundreds of thousands for tiny gains.
