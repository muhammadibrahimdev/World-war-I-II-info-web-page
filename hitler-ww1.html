Hitler's Journey: WW1 and Munich

In 1914 Hitler joined a Bavarian regiment. He worked as a message runner on the Western Front, was wounded, and won the Iron Cross in 1918. In October 1918 a gas attack temporarily blinded him, and he was in hospital when Germany surrendered.

  • 1919: joined a small party that became the Nazi Party
  • 1921: became its leader
  • 1923: Beer Hall Putsch in Munich failed; he went to prison and wrote Mein Kampf
