How WW2 Ended

Soviet troops reached Berlin in April 1945. Hitler killed himself on 30 April 1945, and Germany surrendered in May. After the US dropped atomic bombs on Hiroshima (6 Aug) and Nagasaki (9 Aug), Japan surrendered on 2 September 1945. Roughly 70-85 million people died.
