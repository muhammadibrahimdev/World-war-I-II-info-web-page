Hitler's Early Life

Adolf Hitler was born on 20 April 1889 in Braunau am Inn, Austria. He grew up near Linz. His father died in 1903 and his mother in 1907. He was not a good student, and he wanted to become an artist.

Adolf Hitler in a 1930s official photo

In 1907 he went to Vienna and applied to the Academy of Fine Arts. He was rejected in 1907 and again in 1908, partly because his drawings of people were weak. Then he lived in cheap hostels and a men's home and sold small paintings of Vienna buildings to survive. In 1913 he moved to Munich.
