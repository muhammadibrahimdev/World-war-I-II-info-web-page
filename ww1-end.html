How WW1 Ended

The USA joined in 1917 and Germany ran out of men and food. An armistice (ceasefire) started on 11 November 1918. About 10 million soldiers died.

Painting of the Versailles peace signing, 1919

The Treaty of Versailles (1919) made Germany take the blame, pay reparations, lose land and shrink its army. Many Germans hated it, and later extremist politicians used that anger.
