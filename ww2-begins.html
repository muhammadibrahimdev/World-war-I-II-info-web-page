WW2 Begins

Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939, and Britain and France declared war. In 1940 Germany conquered Denmark, Norway, the Netherlands, Belgium and France with fast 'Blitzkrieg' attacks. Britain held on in the Battle of Britain. In June 1941 Germany attacked the USSR.
