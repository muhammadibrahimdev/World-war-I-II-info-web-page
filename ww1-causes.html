Causes of World War 1

A common way to remember the causes is MAIN: Militarism, Alliances, Imperialism, Nationalism. Europe was divided into two groups, the Triple Entente (Britain, France, Russia) and the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, later the Ottoman Empire).

Map of the European alliances in 1914 (Triple Entente vs Central Powers)

On 28 June 1914 Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria was shot in Sarajevo by Gavrilo Princip. Austria declared war on Serbia, and the alliances dragged the other countries in like dominoes.
